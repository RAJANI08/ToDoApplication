About

The To-Do application is a user-friendly task management system designed to help individuals efficiently plan, organize, and track their daily activities. It enables users to create, update, and manage tasks with ease, ensuring better control over priorities and deadlines. By providing a structured approach to task management, the application enhances productivity, reduces missed tasks, and promotes disciplined time management through a clean and intuitive interface.

Operations supported
  • Adding new tasks
  • Updating existing tasks
  • Deleting existing tasks
  • Searching tasks
  • Data Retrival
